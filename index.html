A little about me

Good songs,
good people.

I’m Zara, a 22-year-old acoustic singer from Reading. I play a mix of pop, folk-pop, indie classics and feel-good favourites — the kind of set that works just as well in a busy pub as it does at a laid-back party. I can shape my set around the venue, event and crowd, offering solo performances with a personal, warm and fun feel.

I also perform as a duo alongside my guitarist, Ryan Paul-Henry, putting our own spin on classic hits including “Wonderwall” by Oasis, “Last Request” by Paolo Nutini and “The A Team” by Ed Sheeran.
